Battery pack temperature

one band per pack, named by serial
waiting for per-pack readings

Two traces to a pack, in one colour because they are one quantity from one battery: its hottest cell solid, its coldest dashed. The hottest cannot sit below the coldest, so position separates the pair and the dash only confirms it. The pack's own temperature_c is read out on hover rather than drawn, because it is not one thing: on a raw sample it is the same number as the hottest cell, the inverter library filling both from a single register, and on a rollup it is the bucket's mean while the drawn pair are the bucket's maximum and minimum. That is also where the pair spreads apart, and why the legend on this section names the tier these bands came from rather than leaving the header to speak for them — per-module readings are kept at full cadence and hourly with nothing between, so the two halves of this page can be drawn from different tiers of the same range. The bank band above is a different register again: the hottest cell anywhere in the bank, not in any one pack, which is exactly what a pack-by-pack chart is here to open up.
